2 Problem Statement

Template Format: [Regional hospital transfusion officers and emergency coordinators] experience [preventable blood stockouts during urgent surgeries while nearby hospitals incinerate expiring units] because [inventory data is trapped in isolated facility silos and transfers rely on frantic, 45-minute phone-call chains without real-time logistics or policy visibility]. This makes it difficult for them to [secure compatible donor blood within the critical golden hour of trauma and obstetric emergencies, leading to preventable maternal and accident mortalities]. We want to explore a solution that helps them [proactively predict shortages, deterministically route units approaching expiration, and approve verified emergency transfers in under 5 seconds with absolute clinical trust and zero hallucination risk].

Project Statement: Blood is a perishable, non-fungible biological resource (packed red blood cells expire in 35–42 days). In regional healthcare networks across Kenya, blood banking faces a tragic paradox: sub-county facilities frequently face acute stockouts during postpartum hemorrhages, while tertiary hospitals 35 km away incinerate viable units expiring without surgical demand. CAELUM eliminates this manual telephony chaos with an autonomous, verified multi-agent platform.

Technical Solution & Execution Roadmap

4 Proposed Solution & Key Features

CAELUM is a Hybrid Multi-Agent System (MAS) combining classical statistics, deterministic transit mathematics, and single-shot AI policy synthesis:

1. Predictive Shortage Forecasting

Supabase stock ledger paired with classical Holt-Winters exponential smoothing to predict depletion up to 72 hours in advance.

2. Deterministic Corridors & RAG

Python logistics worker filters valid transit routes against shelf life; NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Embed searches hospital transfer SOPs via `pgvector`.

3. Single-Shot HITL Approval Inbox

Llama 3.1 8B synthesizes options into an actionable clinical recommendation card, verified by a hard Python validator for 1-click dispatch.

5 Role of Technology & AI (People First)

Why this Tech? React 18 + Tailwind for low-latency emergency triage; Supabase (`pgvector`) for unified relational + vector integrity; Python Statsmodels for auditable forecasting.

Role of AI: AI does NOT calculate math, routes, or inventory. It serves strictly as a natural-language policy synthesizer. The human clinical officer retains 100% authority.

6 Trust, Safety & Data Privacy

Source Transparency: Cites exact mathematical variables and retrieved clauses from hospital transfer SOPs.

Data Minimization: Zero patient PII; tracks only anonymous unit lots and hospital GPS coordinates.

Sovereign On-Prem Ready: Containerized via local NVIDIA NIM, fully compliant with the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019).

8 Potential Limitations & Future Scope

Current Limitations:

Prototype models a regional network of up to 10 facilities focusing on packed RBC and platelets. Travel times use regional velocity matrices rather than live GPS traffic feeds.

Future Roadmap:

Integrate BLE cold-chain temperature sensors inside smart transport boxes; connect to autonomous medical drone APIs (Zipline); federate with Kenya National Blood Transfusion Service (KNBTS).
